# Code of conduct

emu32 is made for students and teachers. Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues,
pull requests or anywhere else, is expected to keep it a place where people can learn and
ask questions without fear.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every question is a fair question.
- Assume good intent. Explain, do not belittle.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when you do.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing someone's private information
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through the e-mail address on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or
open an issue if the matter is not private. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block accounts that break this code.
